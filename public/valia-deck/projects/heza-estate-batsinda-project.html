A Residential Estate, Not Just a Block of Flats

11 HECTARES · 18 CLUSTERS

The official project description positions Heza Estate as a planned neighbourhood for working professionals, families and diaspora buyers. The development combines homes with shared amenities and everyday services inside the estate.

A kindergarten is part of the planned community facilities.

◈

Managed community

RSSB says its facilities team manages the estate initially, with homeowners' association governance thereafter.

Promoter / developerRwanda Social Security Board (RSSB)

Development context

Heza Estate is also referred to in public-sector reporting as Batsinda Phase II. RSSB is the promoter. Government reporting describes a tripartite public-sector arrangement involving RSSB, Rwanda Housing Authority and MINECOFIN, with infrastructure oversight responsibilities separately identified.

Construction & handover signal

The latest detailed public progress update located for this report is from 10 July 2026: RSSB's CEO told Parliament housing construction had reached 97%, infrastructure was 70%, and handover was targeted for January 2027. These are dated reported milestones, not a live site inspection.

Read the price carefully

The two apartment examples are close in price per square metre, even though the three-bedroom apartment has a higher total price. The townhouse models cannot be ranked by price per square metre until RSSB confirms the exact floor area for each model.

Earlier published material shows a different schedule: RWF 80M for the 2-bedroom Umurage townhouse, RWF 98M for the 3-bedroom Iriba townhouse, RWF 101M for 2-bedroom apartments and RWF 111M for 3-bedroom apartments. Treat these as earlier brochure / launch figures, not the current website's featured prices.
Market interpretationUse model-matched comparisons only.

No competitor price ranking is shown here because a reliable, current, same-bedroom and same-property-type comparison set was not established from the available public sources. This avoids mixing apartment and townhouse prices or using stale launch prices as current market evidence.

Neighbourhood position

Heza Estate is in Batsinda, within Kinyinya Sector, Gasabo District. RSSB describes the surrounding area as a growing residential hub with access to schools, healthcare, shopping centres and Kigali's main roads.

BatsindaKinyinya SectorGasabo DistrictKigali City

Access evidence

The public sources reviewed describe nearby services in general terms but do not establish verified gate-to-gate distances to individual schools, clinics, shops or transport stops. This report therefore does not invent walking times or distance figures.

For a buyer-facing access panel, distances should be measured from the verified estate entrance to named destinations using a routable map network, then labelled as walking or driving distance—not straight-line estimates.

Buyer checks before commitment

Confirm the exact unit number, floor area, title / transfer process, service charge, parking allocation, handover condition, utilities, and whether the quoted price remains available.

Availability status

The official site marks some property types as sold out, but it does not provide a complete live inventory feed. Treat individual unit availability as unverified until RSSB confirms it directly.
