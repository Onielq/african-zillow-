02 · Project Scope & Products

Project Scope & Commercial Space

RETAIL · OFFICE · HOTEL · LEISURE

Inzovu Mall is a mixed-use commercial development by Duval Great Lakes / Groupe Duval in Kimihurura. The developer describes an open-air retail destination with office and business-centre space, a 95-room Odalys business-tourism residence, dining, leisure, services and conference-related uses.

The current developer project page lists a Q1 2027 delivery target. Its lower project-history table still includes an older T3 2025 delivery entry for Inzovu Mall. Developer and project team

Developer: Duval Great Lakes / Groupe Duval. Architect: Coldefy. Project collaborator: Vavaki. A third-party project record describes two commercial towers of more than 10 floors; exact floor count and building height in metres were not published in the reviewed material.

A 2023 New Times report quoted an estimated project cost of about USD 68 million (around RWF 81 billion at the time). This is an older reported development-cost estimate, not an audited final cost or current asset valuation. Whole-project market value is not published.

What can be compared safely

Three monthly-labelled market references in the current Plut listing sample are RWF 21,750/m²/month in Kacyiru, RWF 22,400/m²/month at The Rock, and RWF 33,850/m²/month for a 379 m² Kimihurura office. Their mean is RWF 26,000/m²/month. Inzovu Mall’s 600 m² asking rate is RWF 36,750/m², and Brilliant Tower’s 454 m² listing is RWF 32,000/m²; neither listing excerpt states its billing cycle. Do not calculate a premium, discount or monthly outlay for these two listings until the agent confirms the period and included charges.

Project location: Kimihurura, Rugando Cell, Gasabo District, Kigali City, Rwanda. Plot identifier recorded in the Taiseel project register: UPI 1/02/08/03/681. The exact public entrance and postal street number should be confirmed by the developer before routing tenant visits.

The 2023 IFC / ERM environmental and social baseline measured sensitive-receptor distances from the project plot: Kigali Junior Academy 416 m SE, Dove International Montessori 306 m S, University of Kigali 518 m N, Parliament 100 m N, Rwanda Development Board 515 m NE, and Kimihurura Sector Office plus Police Office 150 m E. These are 2023 baseline distances from the project plot, not live walking-route times from the public entrance. The same ESIA proposed a dedicated bus stop immediately outside the mall, subject to coordination with local transport authorities; it does not prove that the stop is operating today. Kigali Bus Services route information lists KCC1, KBC2 and Kigali Heights stops in the wider Kimihurura corridor. The developer’s final public entrance and routed distances remain to be confirmed.
