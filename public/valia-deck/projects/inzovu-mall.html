Project Scope & Commercial Space

RETAIL · OFFICE · HOTEL · LEISURE

Inzovu Mall is a mixed-use commercial development by Duval Great Lakes / Groupe Duval in Kimihurura. The developer describes an open-air retail destination with office and business-centre space, a 95-room Odalys business-tourism residence, dining, leisure, services and conference-related uses.

Area measures are not interchangeable

Developer website: 29,000 m² gross leasable area and project scope over 30,000 m². Architect Coldefy: 27,486 m² net surface. Third-party project record: 26,885 m² plot area. A 2023 press interview described about 40,000 m² built-up area. These figures describe different measures and need a final certified area schedule before valuation.

Space reservation: the official website invites prospective tenants to submit a reservation enquiry. No public reservation deposit, lease deposit, base rent schedule, service charge or payment timetable was found. The enquiry is not proof that a unit is available.

The current developer project page lists a Q1 2027 delivery target. Its lower project-history table still includes an older T3 2025 delivery entry for Inzovu Mall. L2B recorded the project as underway on 1 October 2026. These sources do not establish a signed practical-completion certificate or opening date.

Developer and project team

Developer: Duval Great Lakes / Groupe Duval. Architect: Coldefy. Project collaborator: Vavaki. A third-party project record describes two commercial towers of more than 10 floors; exact floor count and building height in metres were not published in the reviewed material.

A 2023 New Times report quoted an estimated project cost of about USD 68 million (around RWF 81 billion at the time). This is an older reported development-cost estimate, not an audited final cost or current asset valuation. Whole-project market value is not published.
