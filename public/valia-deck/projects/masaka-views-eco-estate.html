What Is Being Developed

382 HOMES · PHASED DELIVERY

Masaka Views is a Fortis Green Housing development in Masaka, Kicukiro District. The wider estate is planned for 382 homes. The developer's website distinguishes homes offered for purchase from apartments planned for rental.

The developer's current sales website shows 3- and 4-bedroom semi-detached family homes and 2- and 3-bedroom townhouses. Rental apartments are listed as coming soon.

Developer

Fortis Green Housing

The project is developed by Fortis Green Holdings / Fortis Green Housing, a US-founded developer operating in Rwanda. The public site describes Masaka Views as its first eco-estate in Rwanda.

Pricing caution

The lowest calculated starting rate in this project’s published sale schedule is approximately USD 617/m² for the 3-bedroom townhouse. The other rates are USD 740/m² for the 2-bedroom townhouse and about USD 900/m² for both family-home types. This is an internal product comparison, not proof of a market discount. Separate 2026 press coverage gives RWF 102M–212M bands for selected units; do not combine those with USD list prices without a dated quotation and exchange-rate basis.

Apartment unit prices are not listed as for-sale products on the developer's current website; the 302 apartments are described as rental stock. Do not present them as purchasable units.

Address & neighbourhood position

Masaka Sector, Kicukiro District, City of Kigali, Rwanda. The estate is near Masaka Health Centre and within the wider Masaka medical-services corridor. The developer markets proximity to Kigali Medical City; this is a planned medical-cluster context, not a completed hospital campus.

Nearby access & verification

Masaka District Hospital and Masaka Health Centre serve the area. Masaka Modern Market includes an integrated bus park, and St Emmanuel School Complex is a named school in Masaka Sector. These are area-level references, not a verified gate-to-gate proximity schedule. A March 2026 report quoted a project representative describing Masaka Hospital as within 500 metres; confirm the actual route and entrance before using the distance in a buyer decision.

Exact estate gate, unit availability, current phase completion and routed distances to schools, healthcare and transport require confirmation with Fortis Green and an on-site check.

Site-visit booking: the developer offers pre-arranged visits and group tours. Its “We Drive” option is advertised at RWF 5,000 per person; group bookings require 8–20 participants. This is a site-tour fee, not a home reservation deposit; tour availability is not guaranteed. Book a tour with Fortis Green.
