01 · Project structure

What Is Being Developed

382 HOMES · PHASED DELIVERY

Masaka Views is a Fortis Green Housing development in Masaka, Kicukiro District. The wider estate is planned for 382 homes. The developer's website distinguishes homes offered for purchase from apartments planned for rental.

CategoryHomesMarket route
Apartments · 1–3 bedrooms302Long-term rental
Single-family homes51For sale
Townhouses33For sale

The developer's current sales website shows 3- and 4-bedroom semi-detached family homes and 2- and 3-bedroom townhouses. Rental apartments are listed as coming soon.

Developer

Fortis Green Housing

The project is developed by Fortis Green Holdings / Fortis Green Housing, a US-founded developer operating in Rwanda. The public site describes Masaka Views as its first eco-estate in Rwanda.

Finishing & layout

The developer lists different finish packages and product configurations. A 2-bedroom townhouse is described as potentially convertible to 3 bedrooms at additional cost. The 3-bedroom townhouse includes a rooftop terrace in its stated gross floor area.

Pricing caution

These are published “starts at” USD prices on the developer's sales pages. Separate 2026 press coverage gives RWF price bands of RWF 102M–212M for selected units. Do not merge those figures into one rate card without a dated unit quotation and exchange-rate basis.

Apartment unit prices are not listed as for-sale products on the developer's current website; the 302 apartments are described as rental stock. Do not present them as purchasable units.

03 · Sustainability & finance

The Eco-Estate Proposition

EDGE · WATER · ENERGY
♧

EDGE-certified

Phase 1 homes received an EDGE preliminary certificate with 25% predicted energy savings, 34% water savings and 21% less embodied carbon in materials.

↻

Recycled water

On-site water treatment is planned to support reuse for estate gardens and the community gardening hub.

☼

Solar options

Solar power is described as an optional extra for buyers, not a standard feature of every unit.

♻

Household recycling

The developer says homes will be supplied with recycling equipment and guidance.

⚕

Wellness

Community activities, fitness and coaching are part of the planned resident programme.

🌱

Garden hub

Food-producing gardens, fruit trees and air-cleaning plants form part of the estate concept.

Published payment options40–40–20 · 40–60 · Mortgage · Cash

The developer lists 40–40–20 for off-plan homes, 40–60 for near-completion homes, full payment and mortgage / construction-loan options through selected banks including BK, NCBA, Equity, Ecobank and BPR. Lender approval and current terms apply.

Delivery update

On 9 October 2026, Fortis Green reported construction at 85%, with water and electricity installed and road works progressing. Remaining first-phase work was described as mainly landscaping and cleaning.

A separate report says 44 first-phase units were available at the time of an August 2026 financing announcement, and remaining first-phase units were expected by mid-2027. Availability and handover timing must be confirmed with the developer.

Location context

The project is in Masaka, Kicukiro District, near the Masaka Health Center / planned healthcare cluster. The developer markets proximity to Kigali Medical City. Individual destination distances should be routed from the confirmed estate entrance.
