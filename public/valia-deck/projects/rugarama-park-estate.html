01 · Project scope & products

What Was Planned

42 HECTARES · MIXED-USE HOUSING

Rugarama Park Estate was planned as a large affordable and mixed-use housing development in Nyamirambo, Nyarugenge District. The joint-venture structure is consistently associated with the Development Bank of Rwanda (BRD), Shelter Afrique and Remote Group, with the City of Kigali contributing the site in project feasibility reporting.

⌂

Housing

Historical plans include stacked townhouses and other residential typologies for lower- and middle-income households.

Timeline signal

Initiated 2015–2017

A 2021 feasibility study says negotiations began in 2015 and formal project start was in 2017. It records site-assembly complexity, promoter financial difficulties and key personnel turnover as causes of delay.

The same study reported that site activity was suspended after Remote Group experienced financial difficulties. That is a historical status, not proof of the current site condition.

A construction-intelligence record updated September 2026 lists the project as “shelved”. Other sources refer to some delivered units and ongoing work. Current activity, ownership and contractor appointment must be confirmed on site and with the JV parties.

What the evidence supports

RHA published an intended price range of RWF 12M–35M for planned housing types. Housing Finance Africa also records that historical range. These are planning-era figures and should not be displayed as current sales prices.

What is not established

No reliable current unit schedule, floor areas, active sales price list, live inventory, total project development cost, independent whole-estate valuation, or confirmed handover programme was established in the reviewed public sources. Price per square metre cannot be calculated responsibly without matched current prices and areas. Total resident capacity and building height in metres are also not established.

Location record

Nyamirambo Sector, Nyarugenge District, City of Kigali, Rwanda. The coordinate shown is an approximate third-party project reference, not a verified legal boundary or entrance pin. Rugarama Primary School and Groupe Scolaire Akumunigo are school references in the wider Nyamirambo area; Nyamirambo Market, Nyamirambo Brigade bus stop and Amaris Medical Clinic are other local access references. Their exact walking distance from the approximate project pin is not verified. Published records conflict on the planned unit count (about 1,700–2,800 across different sources); no verified current resident/occupant capacity or building height is available.

What must be verified

Confirm which JV entity currently controls the project, whether construction is active, which phases have been completed, current unit count, title / parcel references, approved plans, contractor and any units legally offered for sale.

Do not use a historic project announcement as evidence that a unit is available to buy today. Require a site inspection and direct written confirmation from the responsible project entity.
