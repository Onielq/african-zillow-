01 · Project scope & products

What Was Planned

42 HECTARES · MIXED-USE HOUSING

Rugarama Park Estate was an affordable-housing and infrastructure project in Nyamirambo, Nyarugenge District. The clearest approved target is 2,700 planned homes: the City of Kigali’s 4 April 2025 Council resolution confirms 42 hectares of combined land, consisting of 32.9 hectares transferred by the City and 9 hectares acquired by Rugarama Park Estate (RPE) with BRD / Shelter Afrique funding. A July 2025 New Times report separately describes the design site as 30 hectares; these are different land descriptions and should not be merged.

TAISEEL / PROJECT INTELLIGENCE02 / PROJECT SCOPE & PRODUCTS
02 · Development & delivery

Why Delivery Stalled — and What Changed

CITY COUNCIL RESOLUTION · 4 APR 2025

Original project structure

Shelter Afrique and Banque Rwandaise de Développement (BRD) held Rugarama Park Estates Ltd as a 50:50 joint venture. The 2024 Shelter Afrique annual report described the JV as responsible for about 2,700 affordable homes and infrastructure, in collaboration with the City of Kigali. Remote Group later worked with RPE on construction.

What had been built

The City Council’s 4 April 2025 report records 18 structures started with Remote Group: 8 had reached foundation level, 5 had reached the first floor and 5 had reached the second floor. This is a dated construction record, not a current site inspection.

The 2025 land decision

Agreement ended by consent

Council Resolution 80/04.04.2025 approved an amicable ending of the City’s agreement with RPE. The City was to recover its 32.9 hectares; RPE could retain the 9 hectares it had purchased, subject to the City’s master plan.

The resolution identifies two delay drivers: Remote Group’s financial difficulties during COVID-19 and withdrawal of the expected government infrastructure-subsidy arrangement.

On 22 July 2025, The New Times reported that the City was seeking a new developer to revive the project. The report described a 30 ha concept, a USD 50 million project and capacity for up to 14,000 residents. Those are press-reported plan figures, not proof that a new developer was appointed or that the project restarted.

What the price evidence supports

RHA’s historic plan placed selected housing types within RWF 12M–35M. The 2025 City Council record confirms the 2,700-home target but does not update unit prices. Price per square metre cannot be calculated because current unit areas and current sale prices are not published.

What the value evidence does not establish

USD 50 million is a reported project budget / cost, not a current market valuation of the remaining 9 ha, the partly built structures or a completed estate. No current unit schedule, sale availability, lease terms, independent asset valuation, approved new developer, total resident capacity or building height in metres has been verified.

What must be verified

Confirm which JV entity currently controls the project, whether construction is active, which phases have been completed, current unit count, title / parcel references, approved plans, contractor and any units legally offered for sale.

Do not use a historic project announcement as evidence that a unit is available to buy today. Require a site inspection and direct written confirmation from the responsible project entity.
