01 · Project identity

What Was Planned

42 HECTARES · MIXED-USE HOUSING

Rugarama Park Estate was planned as a large affordable and mixed-use housing development in Nyamirambo, Nyarugenge District. The joint-venture structure is consistently associated with the Development Bank of Rwanda (BRD), Shelter Afrique and Remote Group, with the City of Kigali contributing the site in project feasibility reporting.

Timeline signal

Initiated 2015–2017

A 2021 feasibility study says negotiations began in 2015 and formal project start was in 2017. It records site-assembly complexity, promoter financial difficulties and key personnel turnover as causes of delay.

The same study reported that site activity was suspended after Remote Group experienced financial difficulties. That is a historical status, not proof of the current site condition.

A construction-intelligence record updated September 2026 lists the project as “shelved”. Other sources refer to some delivered units and ongoing work. Current activity, ownership and contractor appointment must be confirmed on site and with the JV parties.

What the evidence supports

RHA published an intended price range of RWF 12M–35M for planned housing types. Housing Finance Africa also records that historical range. These are planning-era figures and should not be displayed as current sales prices.

What is not established

No reliable current unit schedule, floor areas, active sales price list, live inventory, or confirmed handover programme was established in the reviewed public sources. Price per square metre cannot be calculated responsibly without matched current prices and areas.

Location record

RHA places the development in Nyamirambo Sector, Nyarugenge District. Housing Finance Africa describes it as just outside central Kigali, close to amenities and public transport.

What must be checked next

Confirm which JV entity currently controls the project, whether construction is active, which phases have been completed, the current unit count, title / parcel references, approved plans, contractor and any units legally offered for sale.

Do not use a historic project announcement as evidence that a unit is available to buy today. A site inspection and direct confirmation from the responsible project entity are needed.
