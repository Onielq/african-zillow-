Typography and hierarchy

Display type is reserved for project names and section headlines. Body type carries evidence, calculations and caveats. Labels stay short and factual.

Evidence and details · Inter / Arial

Developer-reported details are not the same as independently verified facts. State the source and date next to material claims.

Evidence labels

Labels make the strength and origin of a claim visible. Do not turn unknown facts into confident numbers.

Independently verified Developer-reported Government-reported Taiseel-calculated Not published Not established Availability unverified Historical plan only Map context only
An image is not proof of current construction. A map search is not a surveyed boundary. A published price is not a completed transaction. Keep those distinctions visible.

Market comparison and value

Comparable assets must match the relevant use, area basis, currency and price type. Keep sale prices separate from rent, and monthly rent separate from annual rent.

Do not calculate the worth of a whole building from its asking rent or gross floor area alone. A defensible valuation needs a clear method, dated inputs and explicit assumptions.

Two-map location pattern

The first map should help locate the project. The second should help understand the surrounding area and named access points.

Map 1 · Project locationExact pin only when source precision supports it
Map 2 · Surrounding areaSchools · universities · transit · hospitals · City Jewels
City Jewels show relevant buildings and local anchors. Direct comparables are a separate list used for market pricing. Do not present a city-wide reference as if it were next door.

Writing rules

Write like a property analyst: name the fact, name the source, explain the calculation, then state what is not yet known.

Prefer

“The developer reports 40+ launch units. Current available inventory was not published.”

Avoid

“40 units are available now.”

Prefer

“The tower has G+12 floors and two basement levels. A verified height in metres was not published.”

Avoid

Converting floor count into an invented metre height.

Prefer

“The school is in the wider sector; route distance from the project entrance is not verified.”

Avoid

Calling a facility “two minutes away” without a route and method.
